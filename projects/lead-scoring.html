A model that
argues its case.

PPDLS scores sales opportunities — and then explains every single prediction in plain language: model ensemble, SHAP attribution and an LLM explainer, wrapped in an interactive Streamlit app. Built in a weekend at Datathon FME 2025.

Datathon FME (UPC) · Nov 16, 2025 Team entry — modeling & explainability Real business dataset
GitHub ↗ Devpost ↗
The problem

Nobody acts on a score they can't interrogate

Sales teams drown in opportunities and rank them by gut. A model ranks better — but "87% likely to close" convinces nobody. "87%, mainly because of deal size and last-contact recency" starts a conversation. Our rule: every output must survive the question "why?" — asked by someone who's never heard of gradient boosting.

How it works
01

XGBoost and a Random Forest score every lead independently

02

When they disagree, a logistic-regression meta-model adjudicates — disagreement becomes a signal, not a silent failure

03

SHAP attributes each prediction — globally and per lead

04

An LLM writes the explanation a colleague would give, in natural language (structured fallback without an API key)

Explainability, in three layers
GlobalSHAP feature importance per model — what drives the portfolio
Localper-lead SHAP values — what pushed this one up or down
Humanan LLM turns the numbers into the "why" a sales rep will actually read
What I learned

In applied ML, the interface between the model and the human is the product. Two months later my thesis shipped interpretable models to production at Shalion — this weekend build was the rehearsal, on purpose.
