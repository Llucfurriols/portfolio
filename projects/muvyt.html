What it is

A faceless-video studio in the browser

Muvyt generates the entire video from one prompt, then hands you a browser editor — canvas preview, TikTok-style subtitles, per-segment image regeneration and clip animation — to finish the last 10% yourself.

The problem

One video = five tools and hours of grind

Faceless YouTube channels are a real business, but each upload means chaining script, TTS, timestamps, dozens of images and an editor. AI tools automate single steps — nobody automated the whole pipeline for Spanish-speaking creators.

How it works
01

Gemini writes the script from your idea or a YouTube transcript

02

Gemini TTS narrates it — 30 voices to pick from

03

Speech-to-text maps timestamps word by word

04

fal.ai generates one image per ~8s segment (FLUX & friends)

05

ffmpeg renders — and the editor opens for the final touch

Hard parts

Money bugs get database-level fixes

  • An audit found upgrade-cycling could harvest credit allowances — fixed with a partial unique index on the Stripe invoice ID plus a trigger that makes wallet overdraft impossible
  • The signup rate limit silently collapsed all users into one bucket: the proxy masked IPs — solved parsing forwarded-for chains with trusted-hop stripping
  • Two ffmpeg renders can OOM a 2 GB worker — admission control now checks memory before accepting a job
What I learned

Founding something means the model is the easy part. Pricing from token costs, SLOs, load tests, GDPR purges, the support inbox — the buck stops with us, for the math and the invoice.
